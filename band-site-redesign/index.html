About Us

Seven-piece brass, built for the street.

Blastabrass formed out of a shared love of second-line grooves, ska horn lines, and the kind of brass that gets a whole crowd moving. No stage required — we play in the middle of the room, on the street, or wherever the party actually is.

We've powered wedding walk-ins, festival main-stage warm-ups, and Saturday night pub crawls in equal measure, building a set list that swings between original tunes and horn-driven reworks of songs everyone already knows.

Available as a full seven-piece or a stripped-back quartet, depending on your space and budget — get in touch and we'll build the right lineup for your event.
